# ICMat-Forge HOST research assets

244 tasks were preregistered and resolved into accepted or rejected outcomes.
The released HOST portfolio contains 170 unique package/payload identities:

| Class | Prediction | Generation | Support | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| EXACT | 25 | 25 | 28 | 78 |
| SIM_ONLY | 87 | 5 | 0 | 92 |
| Total | 112 | 30 | 28 | 170 |

Quality comes from gates, not quantity: source/license, label semantics, group
split, train-only preprocessing, validation-only selection, frozen baseline,
three seeds, quantization, ONNX checks, golden fixtures, package ABI and hash
identity.

Representative IC-material evidence:

- **P096 SEM segmentation:** public Carinthia, HOST–EXACT, W8; quantized mIoU
  0.7794, Boundary-F1 0.9153, small-defect recall 0.8913.
- **SECOM:** public process/yield replay under its source contract.
- **CMP/PVD:** primarily task maps/contracts, not promoted predictive assets.
- **Advanced packaging:** prediction tasks are mostly SIM_ONLY; source-bound
  knowledge/support assets do not imply real packaging samples.
- **SinterGraph P042:** rejected before GPU because record-level L2 experimental
  targets were absent. Its time-cutoff/PSP contract remains a method artifact.

No wafer-fab customer data, packaging line or prospective enterprise blind test
is claimed.
