# Data card

## Team/laboratory measured anchors

- 281 historical Fluoromax PL spectra used for the named PL evaluation;
- 52 real crucible photographs;
- 67 material records, of which 37 have a usable XRD phase label.

The 37 are labeled material records, not 37 independent XRD spectrum files.
The board PL page contains three representative real spectra for replay; it does
not embed all 281 or acquire new spectra live.

## External and derived sources

- Carinthia SEM and SECOM are public datasets under upstream terms;
- JARVIS values are public computation/DFT, not team experiments;
- Europe PMC text is source-bound by document family and license, but paper text
  is not an independent material measurement;
- physical simulation, fixtures, structural derivations and teachers are
  separate truth classes.

See `data/provenance/`, `DATA_LICENSES.md` and task contracts before reusing any
record or metric.
